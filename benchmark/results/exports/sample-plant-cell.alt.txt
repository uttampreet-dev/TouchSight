Plant cell. 5 parts and 0 lines. Reading from the top left. a, Vacuole. dots. left. The large central store of water, sugars and waste. It keeps the cell firm. b, Cell Wall. outline. right. The rigid outer layer, made of cellulose. It gives the plant cell its shape and protects it. c, Cytoplasm. outline. bottom left. The jelly that fills the cell and holds every organelle. Contains the Nucleus. d, Nucleus. dots. right. Controls everything the cell does and holds the chromosomes. Part of the Cytoplasm. e, Chloroplast. dots. bottom. Holds chlorophyll. This is where photosynthesis makes food from sunlight. End of the diagram.
