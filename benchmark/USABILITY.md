# Eyes-closed location test

**Status: not run.** This file is the protocol and blank recording sheet; it contains no tester observations.

Can a person find a named part by touch and sound alone? Test two people on three diagrams, with five named regions
per diagram, for 30 trials total. Use the student app on the real phone with Wi-Fi off. The tester keeps their eyes
closed throughout each trial. A region counts as found only when the tester selects the prompted region and the app
confirms it within three attempts. Record elapsed seconds from the end of the spoken prompt to confirmation or
giving up.

## Protocol

1. Turn Wi-Fi off and verify the app and all three selected diagrams work on the phone offline.
2. Record the phone model, app/build version, test date, and the three diagram IDs. Select five named regions per
   diagram before testing; use the same targets for both testers.
3. Give the tester 60 seconds to explore each diagram with eyes closed, then begin the quiz. If the phone does not
   speak the prompt, the facilitator reads that same target name verbatim.
4. Do not coach or reveal the answer before the tester gives up. Record found/not found, attempts, elapsed seconds,
   and any reveal sentence. For a miss, seconds is the elapsed time until giving up.
5. Report each tester × diagram score out of five. Treat 4/5 on each tester-diagram pair as the gate; do not hide
   individual misses in a pooled total.
