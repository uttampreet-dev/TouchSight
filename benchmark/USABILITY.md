# Eyes-closed location test

Can a person find a named part by touch and sound alone? Two testers, three diagrams, five named regions each,
Wi-Fi off, the student app opened from the home-screen icon. The tester closes their eyes; the facilitator reads
the quiz prompt only if the phone's own voice is not heard. A region counts as found when the quiz says
"Correct" within three attempts. Record seconds from the prompt to "Correct". Honest sample size: 2 × 3 × 5 = 30
trials. State it as such.

## Protocol

1. Open the diagram, Explore for 60 seconds with eyes closed (orientation), then Quiz.
2. Record each prompt: found / not found, attempts, seconds.
3. Do not coach. If the tester gives up, log "not found" and the phone's reveal sentence.
